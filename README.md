I'm Jonathan Telep, software engineer. Side projects below, mostly tools I use every day.
[Telep IO](https://telep.io) is my LLC, software for local businesses and the projects that grow past a weekend.

## Things I've built
- [Bloomer News](https://bloomer.news): positive AI news every day, always sourced
- [StarterLoot](https://starterloot.com): a free, curated library of AI tools for beginner game developers
- [Let's Talk Statistics](https://letstalkstatistics.com): the latest official figures on prices, federal debt, unemployment and household income, each with its government source
- [Website Checker](https://telep.io/website-check): free check of any public URL for HTTPS, phone setup and whether AI crawlers are blocked
- [AI search readiness check](https://telep.io/ai-check): free check of whether AI search can read a page
- [Website Checkup](https://telep.io/checkup): a ranked report of what to fix first on a site (speed, SEO, mobile, AI search)
- [minesSwept](https://mines.telep.io): one shared 1,000 by 1,000 minesweeper round that everybody digs together
- [Measure your yard](https://measure.telep.io): estimate lawn size from a property address using public county maps
- [AskMentions](https://seo.telep.io): early project to see whether AI answers mention your business
- [Muse connectors](https://muse.telep.io): mail, fax, YouTube summaries and package tracking for Meta Muse
- [BarkMarks](https://bark.telep.io): crowdsourced moments in movies and TV that trigger dogs
- [OSRNG](https://osrng.vercel.app): picks what Old School RuneScape content to do next
